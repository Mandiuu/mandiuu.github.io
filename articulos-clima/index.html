'Nothing to Go Back to': How Climate Change Is Driving Migrants to NYC

A year-long investigation with Jazzmin Jiwa for Columbia Journalism Investigations and Documented. We analyzed more than nine million border apprehension records and crossed them with disaster data to find migrants in New York who fled floods, droughts and hurricanes in Guatemala, Bangladesh and Senegal.
